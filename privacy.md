
# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 27, 2026**

TikTok Growth Manager respects your privacy. This policy explains how the application handles information.

## 1. Information We Access
Depending on the permissions you authorize through TikTok, the application may access:
- TikTok account profile information.
- Publicly available video information.
- Account and video analytics provided by TikTok.

## 2. How We Use Information
We use authorized information to:
- Display account information.
- Show available analytics.
- Help users plan content.
- Track audience growth.

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the application or comply with legal requirements.

## 4. Data Security
We take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no online system can guarantee complete security.

## 5. TikTok Authorization
Users connect their TikTok accounts through TikTok's official authorization process. Users can revoke the application's access through TikTok's account settings.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion
Users may request deletion of their information by contacting the application developer. Information will be retained only as necessary for the application's purposes or legal obligations.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary.

## 8. Contact
For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the application developer through the contact information provided with the application.
