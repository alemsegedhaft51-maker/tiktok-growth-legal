Last updated: November 28, 2026

TikTok Growth Manager respects your privacy. This policy explains how the application handles information.

Depending on the permissions you authorize through TikTok, the application may access account profile information, video information, and analytics provided by TikTok.

2. How We Use Information

We use authorized information to display account details, show available analytics, help users plan content, and track audience growth.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared when necessary to operate the application or comply with legal requirements.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. No online system can guarantee complete security.

5. TikTok Authorization

Users connect their TikTok accounts through TikTok's official authorization process. Users can revoke access through TikTok's account settings.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may request deletion of their information by contacting the application developer. Information will be retained only as necessary for the application's purposes or legal obligations.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the application developer through the contact information provided with the application.
